## What the inventory decides for the atlas

- Daily OHLCV with dated splits exists for the whole pivot population (2024-05-01 → 2026-10-08): every daily condition can be computed point-in-time.
- **No 4-hour, hourly or extended-hours history** for the population (hourly bars for CRE, DKI, HUBC, SXTC only): 4-hour rules are daily proxies; operator-entry and pre-market conditions are untestable.
- **No historical float or borrow:** the company cache is overwritten; borrow and pre-session ledgers start in July–September 2026 and cover the bot's lists only.
- **SEC filings and share counts exist point-in-time for 238 symbols** selected on 2026-10-08 (a biased subset); used only descriptively.
- The Phase 6 ledger and every log from 2026-10-08 on are prospective and excluded by instruction.
