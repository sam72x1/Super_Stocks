  Flat means a state, not a countdown.
- **R5 · the missed winners.** Per window: eligible events whose T−10…T−7 pivot-days are all invalidated, split by
  component (I01 chasing, I03 exploded within 5 sessions, I04 group-candle proxy). Such events cannot reach READY by
  construction. For the six largest missed events: whether all six are invalidated, and how likely that is given
  the share of invalidated events among all missed events (hypergeometric).
- **R6 · classes.** Reproduce the sealed class tables from the frozen model (they must equal `final.json`), then
  ticker-cluster bootstrap CIs (2,000, seed 20261011) for each class precision and for READY_1−READY_2,

  Also check that W_k and the tool flag the same number of rows on every session.
- **R7 · «consistently discriminative».** Defined as: lift lower bound above 1 in development, validation and holdout
  (R7a); for the flags that pass, the matched lower bound above 1 in all three (R7b); and whether any flag beats the
  volatility or drop ranking at equal burden in any window (R7c).

## 1. «شمعة القروب»: the original meaning and the exact claim

**In the sources (traced to the images; the quotes are verbatim).** Faisal uses «القروبات» (retail pump groups) as a validity and risk condition, never as a forecast:

- What it looks like: «القروبات ترفع السهم بشموع غبيه توصل 20٪ تجد شمعه طويله خضرا او حمرا بدون جسم ع فريم 4 ساعات» (X_20260827_checklist_7points, item 6).
- The rule: «الشرط خالي من القروبات» (IMG_0151, TG_1819); «شرط شموعه شموع مضارب مادخل فيه قروبات لعبت بالشموع» (IMG_0497); «الشرط كالعاده مالم تدخل قروبات» (TG_2090).
- Why: «دخول سيوله قروبات او غيره لا انا ولا اكبر محلل يستطيع قراءة الشموع» (IMG_0488) — the candles stop being readable.
- What follows: «دخل قروب غبي رفع السهم من 2.50 ل 3.30 … المضارب ضغط السهم ب 86 الف سهم من 2.80 ل 1.90» (IMG_0316); «سهم تجميع تدخل سيوله قروبات الى الهاويه» ($LABT, TG_1835).
- The words «شمعة قروب» themselves come from a third-party education channel (EDU_20260827_dki_group_candle: a DKI 4-hour candle 3.91 → 5.05, then a collapse, «يربح 5 يخسر 50»). A follower's post (TG_20260905_03, not Faisal): «Jz · Ezra · Icu كلها لقطوها قروبات ع 20٪ وهبطت علينا ودخلنا من جديد».

So the condition means: groups are in, the candles cannot be read, the operator may press the stock down — stay out. It says nothing about whether a +70% leg comes 7–10 sessions later.

**In the study.** I04_GROUP_PROXY = at least one daily candle in T−9…T whose range is at least 20% of the previous close and whose body is at most 25% of the range (`k_groupc`, `a_grp10 ≥ 1`). It is a daily stand-in for a 4-hour shape; group participation itself is not observable in the data. One such candle flags up to ten consecutive pivot-days of the stock.
