- **Intraday-only threshold** — a session whose daily high is ≥ 70% over the prior close outside every detected episode (from the local daily bars; kept apart from the daily-close episodes).

- **Chart decision time** — the session a chart shows, from the date ladder in HISTORICAL_4H_SOURCE_AUDIT.md §2.
- T−10…T−7 counts trading sessions back from ONSET (`faisal_engine/readynow/data/calendar.json`). A decision inside [ONSET, max(peak, D1, +70% close)] of any episode is IN_EVENT and excluded.
