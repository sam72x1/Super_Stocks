## E. The twenty required questions (§25)
1. **Is Candidate Generation actually the first divergence?** Yes under lookahead-safe reconstruction: on 39/41 first dated Faisal days the bot has no candidate, 41/41 no watchlist, 41/41 no READY; the only earlier bot object is near-watch (11/41) which is display-cut and non-persistent. (Level 4.)
2. **Which exact gate on each anchor?** DKI: ANCHOR (two-touch) alone, every session 09-08 → 09-16. SXTC: M2 ceiling (cumulative-adjusted hi52), no single gate restores; all identity gates off → PASS. HUBC: M2 ceiling, same shape; Faisal state UNKNOWN. CRE (negative-direction anchor): M4 range, and the bot admits it 17 sessions later without Faisal.
3. **Same gate in independent controls?** Yes: on the 41-ticker set the first wall is ANCHOR 17 / M3 9 / M4 6 / M2_CEIL 3; on 1,320 control rows ANCHOR is the first wall for 615 (47%) — the rule rejects almost everything sitting on a fresh low, Faisal's names included.
4. **Do the gates measure concepts Faisal uses?** Partly. Collapse-after-explosion (M2 floor, M3 concept), oversold (RSI), small float, short <20k: yes. The two-touch anchor is his *entry* anchor used as *admission*; M4 range, M2 ceiling, M5 floor, SOFT, SCORE, DEPTH: no Faisal concept (`GATE_CONCEPT_MATRIX.csv`).
5. **Supported by direct evidence?** BORROW (<20k) SUPPORTED; M2_FLOOR, M3, M1, RSI_OS, ANCHOR, M14 PARTIALLY (concept direct, number from envelope, or wrong stage).
6. **Unsupported or inferred?** M2_CEIL, M4_RANGE, M4_RISE, M5, SOFT, SCORE, DEPTH, NEAR unsupported; RSI_NOW contradicted by his own READY calls.
7. **Does Faisal selection precede the candidate conditions?** Yes on 20/20 anchor-walled rows (touches 0), on 7/7 positive controls, and in text (ELPW before the low; SMX/KWM on the day of the low). The bot's condition (second touch) is the thing he waits for.
8. **Empirical lead-time distribution?** To +50%: median 7.5 sessions, IQR 2–10.5, range 1–18 (n = 12 of 34 with 20 future sessions; 22 never reached +50% in 20). To +25%: median 2; to +100%: median 9. Not concentrated; not "one week".
9. **Evidence of persistent multi-session attention?** Textual: yes (explicit 3–5 session windows, staged checklists spanning weeks, SMX 21 sessions, DXST 34). Numeric from dated units: insufficient (9/41 multi-dated, consecutive median 1).
10. **Does persistent memory represent Faisal better than point-in-time?** Marginally (first observations 2 → 11 of 41 at N = 10) at a ×2.5 false-positive cost; it does not restore his timing because the admission test itself is placed at his trigger.
11. **Focus distinct from Watch?** Not as dated events; Focus is the persistent list, Watch the first act (`STATE_MODEL_PHASE3_REASSESSMENT.md §1`).
12. **Watch distinct from Ready?** Yes: READY = all validity boxes ticked, trigger pending; reversible (SPRC READY→WATCH).
13. **Ready distinct from Trigger?** Yes: READY precedes the trigger event (sweep or stability + MA10>20) by design.
14. **Entry distinct from Trigger?** Entry is the act that follows the trigger (SXTC READY 09-10 → ENTRY 09-11; second ENTRY 09-24); EXIT separate.
15. **Does a temporal representation reduce the first divergence?** Memory alone: little (above). A representation that admits at touch #1 and defers the two-touch test to the trigger would, by LOGO arithmetic, admit 20 more Faisal rows — and ≈615 control rows; whether it discriminates afterwards is **untested and out of bounds**.
16. **Does removing one gate restore Faisal cases?** Only ANCHOR (20/53), at ×3.06 noise; every other single removal ≤ +1 because the walls stack.
17. **Persistence without feature change?** N = 10: 11/41 first observations, FP 12% → 30%. No.
18. **Features without persistence?** No computed bar feature separates Faisal's names from date-matched names at T or T−k (odds ≥3 in 0/49 window cells; matrix confounded against the pre-registered negatives).
19. **Which explanation survives?** *Combination*: stage order (temporal architecture in its non-memory form) + missing stage/validity concepts, with "wrong gate" as the mechanical carrier (the anchor rule) and evidence artifact bounding the persistence numbers. External information: unknown share.
20. **First causally supported failure?** See `PHASE3_FINAL_VERDICT.md`.
