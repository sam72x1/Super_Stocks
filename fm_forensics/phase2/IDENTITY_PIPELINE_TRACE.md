## Where identity and history are conflated
- There is no identity object. "DKI/SXTC/HUBC" are ticker strings used as provider keys and as `_REJECT_REASONS` keys. A symbol change would create a new ticker with its own (short) history; a reverse split keeps the ticker and rewrites the whole history at the provider.
- Consequently **the only identity-dependent transformation in the READY path is the provider mapping (stage 2) and the split-event lookup in the DQ gate (stage 11)**; everything else depends on the *history representation* delivered for that ticker.
- The replay's ID world (same bars, different ticker label) reproduced world A bit-for-bit on all 4 anchors × 20 sessions (`out/replay_anchors.csv`): identity has **no effect** once the bars are fixed.
